## 小纳森 · Xiaonasen 1.2.3

### 简体中文

录音时按钮及外圈显示红色，图标保持清晰；结束录音或取消后，立即恢复原来的主题颜色。识别和快捷键行为保持不变。

升级前退出程序，保留 settings.json、terms.txt 和 runtime/。下载包不含大型模型，请核对 SHA256SUMS.txt。

### English

The recording button and ring turn red while listening, with a high-contrast icon. Stopping or canceling immediately restores the theme colors. Recognition and shortcuts are unchanged.

Exit before upgrading. Preserve settings.json, terms.txt, and runtime/. Large models are excluded. Verify SHA256SUMS.txt.
